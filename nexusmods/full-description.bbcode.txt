[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Your camp is rich. Now you can be too.[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
Every donation to the Greymane Camp is a one-way trip: the Camp Funds pile up, but there is no way to get silver back when you need it. [b]Withdraw Camp Funds[/b] adds a new option to [b]Carl's menu[/b] at the Camp Provisions Tent that converts Camp Funds back into silver.

[color=#FBBF24][b]Features[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][b]Fair rate, read from the game:[/b] the exact inverse of the game's own donation rate. Silver is counted in copper (100 copper = 1 silver) and since patch 2.01 every copper donated gives 10 Camp Funds, so [b]10 Camp Funds give 1 copper, and 1,000 Camp Funds give 1 silver[/b]. Whatever you donate, you can withdraw back exactly. If a patch changes the donation rate, the withdrawal follows it automatically.
[*][b]Pick the amount[/b] with the game's own quantity slider, with a confirmation, capped at what your Camp Funds allow.
[*][b]Only in Carl's menu:[/b] every other NPC, menu and button works exactly as before, and donating still works as usual.
[*][b]Safe:[/b] the conversion goes through the game's own camp transaction, so the game itself checks your balance and applies the change in one step. Nothing is written to disk and no game file is replaced.
[*][b]Fail-closed:[/b] after a game update, if anything the mod needs has changed, it disables itself and says so in its log instead of guessing.
[/list]
[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]How to use it[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Talk to [b]Carl[/b] at the Greymane Camp (Camp Provisions Tent), choose [b]Withdraw Camp Funds[/b], pick the amount with the slider (counted in copper, like the game's own donation) and confirm. Your Camp Funds go down by 10 × the copper you take: 1,000 Camp Funds for every silver.[/color][/size]

[line]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Requirements[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[list]
[*]Crimson Desert [b]2.04.06[/b].
[*]An ASI loader: [b]Definitive Mod Manager 4.5.1+[/b] already installs one ([i]winmm.dll[/i] in [i]bin64[/i]).
[*][b]Linux / Steam Deck:[/b] add [i]winmm=n,b[/i] to your launch options, e.g. [i]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%[/i] (append it to your existing overrides if you already have some).
[/list]
[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[list=1]
[*][b]With DMM (recommended):[/b] use [b]Mod Manager Download[/b] or import the ZIP, enable [b]CampFundsWithdraw[/b] among the ASI mods and apply.
[*][b]Manual:[/b] copy [i]CampFundsWithdraw.asi[/i] and [i]CampFundsWithdraw.ini[/i] into [i]Crimson Desert/bin64/[/i].
[/list]
To uninstall, disable it in DMM or delete the two files. Your save is not changed by removing the mod.
[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Configuration[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8][i]CampFundsWithdraw.ini[/i] lets you rename the menu entry ([i]MenuLabel[/i]), change the modal texts ([i]Title[/i], [i]Text[/i], with [i]%RATE%[/i], [i]%FUNDS%[/i], [i]%MAX%[/i], [i]%MAXSILVER%[/i]) or turn the mod off ([i]Enabled=0[/i]).[/color][/size]

[line]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]The mod writes [i]bin64/CampFundsWithdraw.log[/i]. It should say [i]ready[/i] at startup and [i]menu: added "Withdraw Camp Funds"[/i] when you talk to Carl. If it says [i]disabled: game version not supported[/i], the game was updated: please report it in the Posts tab or on [url=https://github.com/Marukooh/Camp-Funds-Withdraw/issues]GitHub[/url] with the log attached.[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Compatible with other DMM mods and ASI plugins, since it replaces no files. Mods that change Carl's menu or the camp donation itself may conflict.[/color][/size]
